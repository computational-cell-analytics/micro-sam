# Evaluation Data References for the μSAM2 3D Cell and Neurite Segmentation

3D LM and EM evaluation data of the automatic segmentation benchmark, as shown in `eval-3d-data.png`. One citation for each dataset: the publication, or the data record if there is no publication.


## Fluorescence Cells

### In-domain

- **CartoCell**: Andrés-San Román et al. 2023, [10.1016/j.crmeth.2023.100597](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2023.100597)
- **EmbedSeg Organoid**: Lalit et al. 2021, [10.48550/arXiv.2101.10033](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2101.10033) — The MIDL 2021 version (PMLR v143) has no DOI.
- **PhMamm**: Guignard et al. 2020, [10.1126/science.aar5663](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aar5663)
- **PlantSeg Root**: Wolny et al. 2020, [10.7554/eLife.57613](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.57613)
- **PNAS Arabidopsis**: Willis et al. 2016, [10.1073/pnas.1616768113](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1616768113)
- **Wing Disc**: Paci et al. 2025, [10.1038/s44303-025-00099-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44303-025-00099-7)

### Out-of-domain

- **CShaper**: Cao et al. 2020, [10.1038/s41467-020-19863-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19863-x)
- **MorphoNet Patiria**: Barone et al. 2024, [10.1242/dev.202362](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.202362) — The original data. The curated labels are from MorphoNet 2.0 (Gallean et al. 2025).
- **PlantSeg Ovules**: Wolny et al. 2020, [10.7554/eLife.57613](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.57613)
