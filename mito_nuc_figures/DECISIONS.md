# Mito / nucleus EM generalist: dataset decisions

Running log of the per-dataset decisions for the microSAM2 mito + nucleus EM generalist (training) and its automatic + interactive evaluation.

The work is split into two models (2026-10-16): a mitochondria model and a nucleus model.

### Mitochondria training sampling (2026-10-19, user)

- Every block runs connected components as the pre_label_transform (before the sampler), so a binary or semantic mask
  counts as its instances; label_transform2 still relabels after augmentation.
- MinInstanceSampler everywhere, exclude_ids [0] (+ the block's ignore label), min_size 50 voxels (3D) / 25 px (2D).
  min_num_instances 3, measured per volume on 40 random patches: 2 or 1 where fewer than half of the patches hold 3
  (EM_MITO_SPARSE_VOLUMES: MitoEM v2 macro 1 / jurkat 2, mossy pos0 2, UroCell fib1-1-0-3 2, 5 ProbTEM images 2, HeLa
  STARD3 stard3 1, fasting liver 1858 and 8970 (central box) 1, AIVE 1-3 1); CEM-MitoLab and CellMap use 1.
- Labels on constant raw regions become ignore (the missing-raw transform of the EM neuron datasets, extended to any
  constant value): MitoEM H/R (a saturated 254 region in human z = 190) and MitoVerse MICrONS phase 1 (sections and
  tiles filled with 128 or 255). Whole constant planes are rejected by the blank slice sampler.
- MitoVerse MICrONS phase 1: vol1 and vol2 train whole and validate on nothing, since their last 14 slices (the
  validation slab) hold a missing section that rejects every validation patch; vol0, vol3, vol4 validate.
- Balancing (oversample only): EM_MITO_TRAIN_SAMPLES, 200 x sqrt(training voxels / 250M) samples per source clipped
  to [200, 600]; CellMap 800; tiny sources at ~7 passes per epoch (VNC 450, U2OS 1260, haberl18 14, DenseCell 50);
  ProbTEM 2800; MitoLab every tile; about 8,870 iterations per epoch for hvit_t. MitoLab validation capped at 50
  tiles per group.
- Training config: max 4 objects per sample (all models, also nucleus), 2D batch / z unchanged (14/14, 12/14, 10/10,
  8/8), 140 epochs for hvit_t / hvit_s and 150 for hvit_b / hvit_l (estimated ~78-89 h on 2 x 4 H100), LR
  scheduler patience 15 epochs (factor 0.9), no early stopping.
- Human organoids keep the precomputed neck watershed (finetuning/v2/generalist/em_organelles/preprocess_mito_dataset.py).

### Nucleus training sampling and config (2026-10-19, user)

- Only nucleus labels train (checked per dataset): AIVE folds its 'chromatin' class (the nucleoli) into the nucleus,
  SXT keeps class 2 only, CellMap the 'nuc' class, Perez the nuclei masks.
- Every block runs connected components as the pre_label_transform (SXT maps its classes to the nucleus first).
  MinInstanceSampler everywhere, exclude_ids [0] (+ the block's ignore label), min_size 50 voxels.
- min_num_instances per dataset (EM_NUCLEUS_MIN_INSTANCES): 3 for FAFB, MICrONS, Platynereis, microSAM, Janelia; 2 for
  tumor spheroid, human organoids, CellMap, vEM, OpenOrganelle; 1 otherwise. 7 volumes that never reach their value
  need 1 (EM_NUCLEUS_SPARSE_VOLUMES).
- Balancing (oversample only, EM_NUCLEUS_TRAIN_SAMPLES): OpenOrganelle 1000; CellMap, organoids, SXT, Janelia 800;
  Macrophage 424, MICrONS 414, BetaSeg 400, FAFB 293, microSAM 200, other 3D 200, Cerebellum 175; 2D vEM 2800,
  WAE-Net TEM 2500, tumor spheroid 1400, cardiomyocyte 575, WAE-Net ds3 510, Perez 350; at least one sample per volume.
  About 910 iterations per GPU per epoch for hvit_t on 8 GPUs (AIVE moved to evaluation).
- Training config: max 4 objects per sample, 160 epochs for hvit_t / hvit_s and 190 for hvit_b / hvit_l (estimated
  ~85-89 h on 2 x 4 H100), LR scheduler patience 20 epochs (factor 0.9), no early stopping.
